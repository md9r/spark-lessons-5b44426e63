💡 Tricks for Each Table

6️⃣ The 6s

  • 6 is 5 + 1. Do the 5s, then add one more group: 6 × 7 = 35 + 7 = 42.
  • 6 times an even number (2, 4, 6, 8): the answer ends with that same number, and the first digit is half of it. 6 × 4 = 24, 6 × 6 = 36, 6 × 8 = 48.
  • Double the 3s: 3 × 7 = 21, so 6 × 7 = 42.

7️⃣ The 7s

  • You already know lots of them! 7 × 6 is the same as 6 × 7. Flipping the order doesn't change the answer.
  • 5, 6, 7, 8: 56 = 7 × 8. The numbers count in order!
  • 7 is 5 + 2. 7 × 6 = 30 + 12 = 42.

8️⃣ The 8s

  • Double, double, double! 8 × 7: double 7 is 14, double 14 is 28, double 28 is 56.
  • Ones-digit pattern: the answers end in 8, 6, 4, 2, 0, then repeat: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56…
  • 8 is 10 − 2. 8 × 6 = 60 − 12 = 48.

9️⃣ The 9s

  • Digits add to 9: for 9 × 2 up to 9 × 10, the two digits add up to 9: 18 (1 + 8), 27, 36, 45…
  • One less, then fill to 9: 9 × 7 → the first digit is 7 − 1 = 6, and 6 + 3 = 9, so 63.
  • 9 is 10 − 1. 9 × 12 = 120 − 12 = 108.

🖐️ The 9s Finger Trick (9 × 1 to 9 × 10)

Hold up all 10 fingers. To find 9 × 4, fold down your 4th finger. Count the fingers to the left (that's the tens) and to the right (that's the ones): 3 and 6 make 36! Tap a finger to try it.
